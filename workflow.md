# Workflow Guide

1. **Item master:** Create each stock item with a stock UOM of `Nos` and an IT Equipment item group.
2. **Receipt:** Use a material receipt to place 12 laptops and 12 docks in Central Warehouse.
3. **Transfer:** Transfer eight of each item to Field Staging and retain the linked stock-entry record.
4. **Physical count:** Count devices at Field Staging. The sample count finds seven laptops instead of eight.
5. **Reconciliation:** Enter the variance only after confirming the count; include a short reason and retain the audit trail.
6. **Review:** Report the variance to the operations owner, then investigate issuance records before ordering replacements.
